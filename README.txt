# 3D Pursuer-Target Engagement Simulator

This project is a pursuer-target engagement simulator developed to evaluate and compare classical guidance laws in three-dimensional interception problems. The study focuses on a simplified solid-propellant rocket interceptor designed to engage one-way attack drones.

The implemented guidance laws are:

- Pure Pursuit (PP)
- True Proportional Navigation (TPN)
- Augmented Proportional Navigation (APN)
- Pure Proportional Navigation (PPN)

The simulator compares the performance of these guidance laws under different modeling and implementation constraints, including target lateral acceleration, discrete guidance command updates, lateral acceleration saturation of the pursuer, and variable acceleration of the pursuer (a simplified representation of a solid-propellant rocket motor).

This work was developed between 2025 and 2026 as part of my undergraduate final thesis in Aerospace Engineering at the Federal University of Santa Maria.
